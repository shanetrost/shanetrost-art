2. What this policy covers

The site at shanetrost.art, a portfolio of paintings and design work. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to buy.

3. What we collect

Usage data. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which work people look at. It collects pages viewed, time on page, referring source, browser and device type, and approximate location derived from your IP address at the city or region level.

Email you send. If you write to an address published on the site, we have your message and your address. Nothing on this site captures it automatically.

A second, lighter analytics measure. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and basic performance data without cookies and without building a profile of you.

Assets loaded from other companies. This site's pages were built with Squarespace's tooling and still load images, fonts and scripts from Squarespace's content delivery network, from Adobe Typekit, and from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those files directly from those companies, which means they receive your IP address and browser type. We do not send them anything else about you.

Server logs. Our hosting provider automatically logs requests including IP address, timestamp and user agent, for security and reliability.

That is all.

4. What we do not collect

There are no forms on this site. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or any other contact information unless you choose to email us. We run no advertising pixels, no retargeting, and no visitor identification. We do not buy personal data about you from third parties. We do not sell work through this site, so we never handle payment information.

5. How we use what we collect

Analytics data tells us which pieces people spend time with. Log data keeps the site running. If you email us, we use your message to reply.

6. We do not sell or share your personal information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California law.
